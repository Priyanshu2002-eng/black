Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026.

What Black stores

This policy covers Black on the web and Black for iPhone. Your birth date, habits, targets, dates and focus sessions are saved only on your device: in your browser's local storage on the web, and in the app's own storage on iPhone (shared with its widgets). They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Black has no accounts and no server that receives your data. The web and iPhone versions do not sync with each other.

What Black does not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, no third-party scripts or SDKs. On iPhone, notifications are used only to tell you when a focus session ends, and only if you allow them.

Your control

On the web, Settings lets you export everything as a file, import it on another device, or erase it; clearing your browser's site data also removes it. On iPhone, Settings lets you erase everything, and deleting the app removes its data.

Hosting

The company that hosts this site may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) when the app's files are downloaded. Those logs do not contain anything you enter in Black.
